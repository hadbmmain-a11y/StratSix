Privacy Policy

StratSix – iBT · Last updated: 19 November 2026

StratSix – iBT is an independent TOEFL iBT® preparation course run by an English teacher based in Bolivia (“the teacher”, “we”). It is a companion to live classes. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written in plain English on purpose.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Name and email from your Google account, when you sign in with GoogleTo create your student account and show your teacher who submitted work. We receive only your name and email address, never your Google password.
Course progress (days completed, practice scores, test results)To unlock the next day, show your progress and let your teacher follow your work.
Speaking recordings and transcriptsSo you and your teacher can listen to and review your answers, and to give feedback.
Writing responses and the self-scores you enterTo give feedback and scores on your writing.
Feedback and scores (from the teacher and from AI)To show you how you are doing and how to improve.

We do not collect payment details, phone numbers, home addresses, location, or government IDs. The site shows no advertising and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How your information is used

  • To run the course: sign-in, progress tracking, practice, tests and feedback.
  • For your teacher to review work and give scores and comments.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for marketing.

3. Who can see your information

  • You can see your own data.
  • The teacher can see all students’ progress, recordings, writing and scores.
  • Other students cannot see your data.

4. Service providers that handle data for us

  • Supabase stores accounts, progress, submissions and recordings in a database with access rules, so each student can only read their own records.
  • Google provides the “Sign in with Google” service.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website pages.
  • AI scoring. To give you feedback, the teacher may send your written responses and speaking transcripts to an AI assistant (Claude, from Anthropic) to produce a practice estimate. Audio files are not sent for AI scoring. AI scores are estimates only and the teacher may review them.
  • Your browser. Speaking tasks use your browser’s microphone and speech features. Depending on your browser (for example Chrome), speech recognition may be processed by the browser’s provider under its own privacy policy. You are asked for microphone permission before anything is recorded.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a student in the course. If you leave, or ask us to delete it, we will remove your account, progress, recordings and writing.

6. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you your data, correct it, or delete it. Write to hadbm.main@gmail.com. You can also revoke StratSix’s access to your Google account in your Google Account settings under “Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services”.

7. Students under 18

The course is intended for students taking TOEFL preparation classes with their teacher. If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian knows you use this site. A parent or guardian can contact us to review or delete a student’s data.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by sign-in and access rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
